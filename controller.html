How to play Open World

LEFT STICK · MOVE
Walk, run, and steer. Push it up to move forward.
RIGHT STICK · LOOK
Move it to look around or aim the camera.
× · JUMP
Jump on foot. It becomes the handbrake in a car.
△ · INTERACT
Talk, use objects, enter or exit a vehicle.
□ · RELOAD
Reload your current weapon. In a car it toggles cockpit view.
○ · CHANGE VIEW
Switch camera view. In a car, use it for the map.
L2 / R2
Aim and fire on foot. Brake/reverse and accelerate in a car.
R1 / L1
Sprint and weapon swap on foot. R1 is handbrake while driving.
TOUCHPAD · MAP
Open the full city map.
GARAGE · MENU
Garage opens the vehicle selector. Menu pauses or resumes the game.
